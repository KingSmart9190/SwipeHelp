How SwipeHelp works

Three steps. Real impact. Full transparency.

01
📝

Post a real need

Upload your expense document — tuition invoice, hospital bill, or utility statement. Our team verifies it within 24 hours.

02
👆

Swipe to discover

Donors browse verified need cards like a dating app. Swipe right to help, left to skip. No endless scrolling.

03
💳

Pay directly

Funds go straight to the school, hospital, or landlord — never to the individual. Fraud-proof by design.

Safety first

Built on verified trust

Every need goes through three layers of verification before donors ever see it.

🪪

Verified Identity

Government ID + phone number required for every poster on the platform.

📄

Verified Need

Utility bills, tuition invoices, and hospital receipts reviewed by our moderation team.

🏫

Direct Payment
