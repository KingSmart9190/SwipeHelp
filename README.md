# SwipeHelp

For SwipeHelp, I'd position it as a social impact platform that combines crowdfunding with the simplicity and engagement of swipe-based discovery.

💙 SwipeHelp

Discover real needs. Change a life with a swipe.

SwipeHelp is a community-driven platform that connects people in need with individuals willing to help. Users can post genuine needs, whether it's school fees, medical assistance, groceries, emergency support, or community projects, while supporters can browse through requests using a simple swipe-based experience and contribute directly to causes that resonate with them.

Every swipe has the power to make a difference.

🌍 What Is SwipeHelp?

SwipeHelp reimagines giving by making it more personal, transparent, and accessible.

Instead of searching through endless fundraising campaigns, supporters can quickly discover real stories one at a time, learn about people's needs, and decide whether they want to help.

Think of it as a modern blend of social discovery and charitable giving, designed to connect generosity with genuine need.
